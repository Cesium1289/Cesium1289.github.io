About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate student at Oregon State University, pursuing an M.Eng with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. My capstone project at Portland State gave me hands-on, full-stack experience building and testing an API-driven web application for a real nonprofit client, and I've since kept building projects that stretch across the stack — from backend systems and databases to game development.

Before and during school, I spent 6+ years leading cross-functional teams in fast-paced, guest-facing environments as an event and banquet manager — work that sharpened my communication, problem-solving under pressure, and ability to translate stakeholder requirements into results on a deadline. I'm a fast learner who's comfortable owning a problem end-to-end, whether that's a line of code or a 1,100-guest event.
